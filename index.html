Before we make something
A few helpful details.
Can I request something that isn’t in the collection?
Yes. Share your idea or a reference with Dimple. She’ll discuss what is possible and confirm materials, design and pricing with you.
How much does a custom piece cost?
The quotation depends on the piece, size, materials, personalisation and quantity. Ask for a quotation before confirming your order.
Can I order for a particular date?
Include your preferred date in the enquiry. Making time and delivery arrangements need to be confirmed with Dimple before you place an order.
Are the pictures finished products or design ideas?
The finished-creations collection shows existing work. Three inspiration designs are kept in the separate Design ideas section and labelled as concepts.
